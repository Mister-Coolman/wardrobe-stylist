Wardrobe Stylist
UI Style Guide

Direction: The Lookbook. The app should feel like a printed fashion lookbook or a tailor’s notebook, not a SaaS dashboard. Your clothes are the color; the interface is paper and ink.

01

Principles

Four ideas every screen follows. When a default (Tailwind, a component library, an AI suggestion) disagrees with these, these win.

Your clothes are the color.

The interface is near-monochrome. Garment photos and renders are the only saturated things on screen.

Editorial, not decorative.

One serif moment per screen. Hierarchy comes from size, weight and space — not boxes, shadows and gradients.

Tailoring is the visual language.

Garment tags, care labels and lookbook numbering (“Look 01”, “Piece 2/4”). Only a wardrobe app can use it — that’s why it’s ours.

Show the thinking honestly.

The stylist’s checks and revisions appear as short “fitting notes”, never as a glowing AI badge.

Logo-swap test: if you replaced our logo with a fintech logo and the screen still made sense, it’s too generic.

02

Proportion on a typical screen

Paper ~60% · studio ~20% · ink ~12% · rules ~5% · accent ≤2% · status ≤1%. Garment photos supply the rest of the color.

07

What we’re avoiding

The same moment — “here’s your outfit” — done the default AI way and our way.

✨ AI-POWERED STYLING
Unlock Your Perfect Look
Supercharge your wardrobe with seamless AI
Smart Outfits
Weather AI
Calendar Sync
Get Started →

Don’t. Gradient text, purple glow, Inter, three equal icon cards, sparkles, “unlock / supercharge / seamless”, pill CTA with chevron. Swap the logo and it’s any app.

Motion & voice

fast / base / sheet150 / 220 / 360 msPress · most transitions · sheets

Signature moments only: scan cutouts settle into the grid one by one; outfit pieces slide into the look (shoes last); a rejected piece strikes through and its replacement slides in. Everything honors reduced motion.

Voice

Say

“Rain from 5pm — swapped to the leather boots.”

“You own 3 clean tees for 4 days.”

“Your closet is empty. Film it once — about a minute.”

Don’t say

“Optimized for weather conditions ✨”

“Unlock your perfect wardrobe with AI”

“Get started on your style journey!”

09

Screen checklist

  1. Squint: one focal point, one primary action.
  2. Logo swap: would it fit a fintech app? Then it’s too generic.
  3. Grayscale: hierarchy survives without color.
  4. Ban list: zero unexplained hits.
  5. States: empty, loading, partial, error.
  6. Phone: 375px, thumb reach, 44px targets, safe areas.
  7. Accessibility: AA contrast, focus rings, no color-only status, reduced motion.
  8. Real content: long names, 1 item vs 80 items.
  9. Screenshot light and dark; compare with neighboring screens.
